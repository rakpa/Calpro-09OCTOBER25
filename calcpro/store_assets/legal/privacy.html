Privacy Policy for Calcara

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Calcara (“we”, “us”) provides a calculator utility app for iOS (bundle ID www.calpro.app).

Information we store on your device

  • App preferences (theme, haptics, sound, tips)
  • Favorites, recent searches, and calculation history
  • Optional local “unlimited history” preference

These stay on your device unless you clear app data or uninstall. We do not operate a Calcara account system.

Microphone & speech (optional)

If voice search is enabled in a future update, the microphone may be used only while you search. Audio is processed to recognize search terms and is not used to build an advertising profile.

Third parties

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Apple / App Store may process download and purchase data under Apple’s privacy policy.
