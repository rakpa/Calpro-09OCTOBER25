Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026 · Bundle ID www.calpro.app

Calcara (“we”, “us”) provides a calculator utility app for iOS.

Information we store on your device

  • App preferences (theme, haptics, sound)
  • Favorites, recent searches, and calculation history
  • Optional local “unlimited history” preference (Calcara Plus)

These stay on your device unless you clear app data or uninstall. We do not operate a Calcara account system.

Microphone & speech (optional)

If voice search is used, the microphone may be accessed only while you search. Audio is processed to recognize search terms and is not used to build an advertising profile.

Third parties

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Apple / the App Store may process download and purchase data under Apple’s privacy policy.
